# PairOnce community

The public place to ask questions, suggest features and report problems with [PairOnce](https://paironce.com), the
browser-to-browser file transfer between your devices.

**[Open the discussions →](https://github.com/paironce/community/discussions)**

- **Q&A**: how do I…, why won't my devices connect, which browser to use.
- **Ideas**: what you would like PairOnce to do next.
- **General**: problems you ran into, with your two devices, browsers and network (Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPN).
- **Announcements**: new releases, from us.

Before posting, the [transfer guide](https://paironce.com/transfer-guide) and
[troubleshooting](https://paironce.com/transfer-troubleshooting) answer the most common questions.

## Not here, please

- **Payments, refunds and license codes** (PairOnce Pro): write to [support@paironce.com](mailto:support@paironce.com)
  with your receipt. Never post a license code or receipt in public.
- **Security problems**: see the [security policy](https://github.com/paironce/security) and write to
  [security@paironce.com](mailto:security@paironce.com), not to a public discussion.

## Be kind

Be respectful, stay on topic, and do not post anything private about yourself or anyone else. Spam, ads and abuse are
removed. Use of PairOnce is covered by its [Terms of Service](https://paironce.com/terms) and
[Privacy Policy](https://paironce.com/privacy).
